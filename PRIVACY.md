# Privacy policy (draft, 2026-09-29)

Controller: Vinh Nguyen (VYDE), contact vyde.apps@gmail.com.

**We don't run servers and we never receive what you ask.** The app has no analytics, no ads and no
crash reporting of its own.

## What happens to your data

| Data | Where it goes | Why | Kept |
|---|---|---|---|
| History: your words and Buddy's replies, in voice conversations and the chat | Encrypted on your phone. A new conversation starts with the last ones, so they go to OpenAI again with it, with codes, numbers and links taken out; what only the phone's own AI heard never does. What your phone read out to you itself is never kept | To remember what you talked about | 30 days, or until you delete it in History; you can turn it off there |
| What you ask Buddy to remember ("remember that…") | Encrypted on your phone; goes to OpenAI with each conversation, so ChatGPT knows it | You asked for it | Until you tell Buddy to forget it or tap Forget in History |
| Questions sent to ChatGPT (only if you turn it on and sign in) | Directly from your phone to OpenAI, under your own ChatGPT account | To answer you; you asked for it | OpenAI's terms and your ChatGPT data settings apply |
| Voice conversations (watch or phone) | Sound goes from the watch to your phone over Bluetooth, and from your phone to OpenAI under your own ChatGPT account, only while a conversation is on | To talk with you | The sound isn't stored; the words are kept in History (above) |
| What's on an app's screen (only if you switch on Buddy in Android's accessibility settings) | Read on your phone when you ask Buddy to do something in an app you turned on for it. ChatGPT only gets the buttons' short labels to plan the steps; what's on the screen (chats, emails) is read by Gemma on your phone and said by your phone. The phone's settings, app stores, password, sign-in code and payment apps are never looked at | To use apps for you | Not stored |
| Which apps Buddy may use | Only the apps you turn on ("Apps Buddy can use"); every other app is off. Your choices, and Gemma's check of an app's name (so a banking app can only be turned on by hand), stay on your phone | So Buddy only goes where you let it | Until you delete everything |
| "Hey Buddy" | Heard on the watch only, in its memory, while it listens: a short while after you raise your wrist, or all the time if you switch that on | To hear the phrase | Not stored; nothing leaves the watch until it hears "Hey Buddy" |
| Learn my voice | Five short recordings, handled on the watch; only what the model heard (a short spelling) is kept | To hear your voice better | Recordings deleted right after; the spelling until you choose Forget my voice |
| Messages you got (only with notification access) | Read from your notifications, kept in the phone's memory for at most 6 hours; read out only when you ask about them. With "Private things stay on this phone" (on unless you turn it off) they never go to OpenAI: Gemma reads them on your phone and your phone says the answer in its own voice (Android's offline speech engine), or reads them out itself without the offline model; ChatGPT only hears what you asked. With it off, they go to OpenAI with codes, card and account numbers, passwords, links, phone numbers and email addresses taken out. Only messages from the apps you turned on for Buddy are read; password manager, authenticator and payment apps never | To read and answer your messages | Never stored |
| Contacts (only with your OK) | Read on your phone when you name someone to text or call | To find the right person | Not stored |
| Texts and calls you confirm | Sent or placed by your phone after your yes; a text reaches the other person through your carrier | You asked for it | Your phone's own message and call history |
| Notes and calendar | Notes encrypted on your phone; calendar events read and added in your phone's calendar (with your OK) | You asked for it | Read on your phone the same way as messages: with "Private things stay on this phone" on, never sent to OpenAI; notes you add go with your request, since you say them to ChatGPT |
| Places for directions, apps you open | Handed to Google Maps or the app on your phone | You asked for it | Their terms apply |
| The small map in Buddy's pop-up (when you ask for directions) | The place name goes from your phone to OpenStreetMap, to find it and fetch the map around it; they see your IP address | To show you where it is | Not stored by the app; OpenStreetMap's privacy policy applies |
| ChatGPT sign-in (tokens, account id, plan, masked email) | Encrypted on your phone with a key that can't leave it | To keep you signed in | Until you sign out or delete everything |
| On-device model download | Your phone downloads it from Hugging Face; they see your IP address | To run Gemma on your phone | The model stays until you delete it |
| Gemini Nano (only if you opt in) | Google's ML Kit sends Google anonymous diagnostics (device model, speed, error codes) | Needed by Google's on-device API | Google's terms apply |
| Answers you flag | Encrypted on your phone; your question only if you tick the box | To report harmful or wrong answers | Until you delete everything |
| Buddy Plus (in builds that offer it) | When the app starts, it asks RevenueCat whether you have Plus and what it costs; RevenueCat sees an anonymous id it makes for this install, the app version, basic device info and your IP address. If you buy, the store takes the payment and RevenueCat records the purchase | To sell Plus and bring it back on a new phone | RevenueCat's and the store's terms; nothing from your conversations goes to them |
| What Buddy did | Encrypted on your phone: the kind of action (a text, a timer) and how it ended, never what was in it | So you can see what Buddy did | 30 days, or until you clear it or delete everything |

International transfers: OpenAI, Hugging Face, Google and RevenueCat may process data in the USA, and the
OpenStreetMap Foundation in the UK (its map tiles come through a worldwide CDN), under their own
safeguards; this happens only when you use those features.

Messages you got and your contacts include other people's details. They're handled on your phone,
only when you ask, and never reach us.

## Your rights

Everything is on your phone, so you control it directly: sign out, delete the model, or
**Settings → Delete everything**. For anything else (access, deletion, complaints) write to
vyde.apps@gmail.com. You can also complain to your data protection authority.

Legal basis: your requests (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) for answers; consent (Art. 6(1)(a), § 25 TDDDG) for
Gemini Nano's diagnostics. Storing the sign-in on your phone is strictly necessary for the service.

The app uses AI to generate answers. They can be wrong. Each answer shows which AI produced it.
