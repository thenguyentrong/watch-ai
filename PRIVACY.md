# Privacy policy (draft, 2026-09-27)

Controller: Vinh Nguyen (VYDE), contact vyde.apps@gmail.com.

**We don't run servers and we never receive what you ask.** The app has no analytics, no ads and no
crash reporting of its own.

## What happens to your data

| Data | Where it goes | Why | Kept |
|---|---|---|---|
| Your questions and answers (chat) | Stays in the app's memory | To show the conversation | Until you close the app |
| Questions sent to ChatGPT (only if you turn it on and sign in) | Directly from your phone to OpenAI, under your own ChatGPT account | To answer you; you asked for it | OpenAI's terms and your ChatGPT data settings apply |
| ChatGPT sign-in (tokens, account id, plan, masked email) | Encrypted on your phone with a key that can't leave it | To keep you signed in | Until you sign out or delete everything |
| On-device model download | Your phone downloads it from Hugging Face; they see your IP address | To run Gemma on your phone | The model stays until you delete it |
| Gemini Nano (only if you opt in) | Google's ML Kit sends Google anonymous diagnostics (device model, speed, error codes) | Needed by Google's on-device API | Google's terms apply |
| Answers you flag | Encrypted on your phone; your question only if you tick the box | To report harmful or wrong answers | Until you delete everything |

International transfers: OpenAI, Hugging Face and Google may process data in the USA under their own
safeguards; this happens only when you use those features.

## Your rights

Everything is on your phone, so you control it directly: sign out, delete the model, or
**Settings → Delete everything**. For anything else (access, deletion, complaints) write to
vyde.apps@gmail.com. You can also complain to your data protection authority.

Legal basis: your requests (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) for answers; consent (Art. 6(1)(a), § 25 TDDDG) for
Gemini Nano's diagnostics. Storing the sign-in on your phone is strictly necessary for the service.

The app uses AI to generate answers. They can be wrong. Each answer shows which AI produced it.
